beatsync

cloud backup for your Beat screenplays.

Beat keeps your scripts on your device. That's great for privacy — but a stolen laptop or a dead hard drive takes your work with it. beatsync watches your scripts folder and silently backs up every change, encrypted end-to-end, so your screenplays are safe even when your Mac isn't.

Set it up once. Never think about it again.

  1. Download beatsync and install it on your Mac.
  2. Create a free account — just an email and a password.
  3. Point it at your Beat folder — the folder where Beat saves your .fountain files (usually ~/Documents/Beat).
  4. Generate your encryption key and save it somewhere safe. This is the only thing beatsync can't recover for you.
  5. Write. Every change is encrypted and backed up the moment you hit save.

Questions, answered.

Does this work with Beat's .fountain files?

Yes — beatsync is built around Fountain. Point it at the folder Beat uses and it handles everything automatically. It also works with any other file types you throw at it.

Beat stores files locally for privacy. Does beatsync break that?

No. Your files are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded, using a key that only you hold. The server stores ciphertext — meaningless without your key. It's the same privacy model Beat uses locally, extended to the cloud.

What if I lose my encryption key?

Your locally-saved files are always intact — beatsync doesn't touch them. The key only matters for restoring from the cloud. We can't recover it for you, so keep a copy somewhere safe when you set up.

What happens to my scripts if I lose my Mac?

Install beatsync on a new machine, sign in, enter your encryption key, and everything is pulled back down. Every version that was ever synced is there.

Does it work with other screenwriting apps too?

Absolutely. beatsync watches any folder and backs up any file types you specify. If your app saves to disk, beatsync can protect it.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. beatsync is free and open source — same philosophy as Beat.
